SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES05
MEASURE

Recorded behavior under a declared protocol

  • Participation and response opportunities
  • Public and private position change
  • Role fidelity and report workflow
  • Infrastructure-adjusted outcomes
DO NOT INFER

Hidden mental or human social states

  • Belief or consciousness
  • Stable personality
  • Human conformity or motive
  • General model quality

RUN-LEVEL INFERENCE Messages inside one workshop share context. Analysis avoids message-level pseudoreplication.

PRIVATE ISOLATION Checkpoints and isolated reviews remain outside unauthorized context.

DECLARED FAULTS Injected failures belong to the frozen configuration and receive a distinct fingerprint.

NO EARLY STOP The host completes scheduled opportunities even when apparent consensus emerges.

# Phase 1A Role Registry

Version: `functional-v1` (calibration instrument)

| ID | Function | Positive evidence | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| proposer | Develops concrete proposals and mechanisms | proposal plus mechanism/tradeoff | generic opinion without a proposal |
| critic | Tests assumptions and counterarguments | specific criticism tied to an argument | performative opposition |
| evidence | Separates support, uncertainty and evidential limits | qualified evidence assessment | fabricated sources or certainty |
| integrator | Connects compatible arguments while retaining disagreement | accurate synthesis with minority preservation | invented consensus |
| consequences | Examines feasibility, stakeholders, reversibility and unintended effects | concrete constraint/tradeoff | unsupported professional or lived-experience claim |

These are functional treatment instructions. They do not assert that a model has
the corresponding personality, identity, expertise or latent belief.

# AGORA Behavioral Rubric

Version: `behavioral-v2` calibration adaptation.

The rubric adapts construct and coding ideas from DQI, CACS and DCS for AGORA's
role-treatment context. Those sources provide provenance for constructs and
coding choices; this is not an externally validated AGORA instrument.

Role criteria are ordinal 0/1/2 with `not_assessable` distinct from absent.
Reviewers must quote the source message, explain the evidence and identify the
stage window. ICR is positive only when a later participant message depends on a
specific earlier peer argument actually present in the authorized context;
mention alone is insufficient. Direct reply and relation subtype are separate.

No label may be interpreted as persuasion, agreement, intelligence,
consciousness, personality or causal influence.

# AGORA Phase 1A Scientific Contract

Status: frozen for calibration, not a scientific validation certificate.

Phase 1A tests one role-treatment manipulation while holding provider, model,
deployment, parameters, tools, moderator, topic, schedule, budgets and topology
fixed. The unit of comparison is a paired block (one control and one treatment
run); messages are nested observations and are never treated as independent
replicates.

The five functional constructs are proposer, critic, evidence, integrator and
consequences. They are task instructions, not personality, demographic or
psychological claims. Assignment is deterministic and cyclic; it is not
randomized.

The canonical sequence is S1 independent initial position, S2 public plenary I,
S3 private breakout, S4 reconvening, S5 public plenary II, S6 independent final
position, S7 sealed ballot and S8 deterministic schema-bound output. S6 must be
complete before S7. S7 and S8 are excluded from behavioral interaction scoring.

Primary outcome: Role Fidelity (RF). Key secondary: Interaction Contingency
Rate (ICR). Secondary: participation inequality (Gini) and initial-to-final
expressed-position change. Voting, structural minority preservation, output
fidelity and relation subtypes are descriptive or exploratory.

RF/ICR labels use independent human review. Krippendorff alpha is calculated
before adjudication; RF and ICR require alpha >= 0.80 and at least 30 pairable
units per stratum. Relation alpha is reported but does not gate the primary
instrument. Reliability is not construct validity.

The only scientific states after engineering closure are
`PENDING_HUMAN_VALIDATION` and, after complete independent review, empirical
reliability and adjudication, `CONFIRMATORY_READY`. Phase 1B is inactive until
the latter transition is explicitly recorded.

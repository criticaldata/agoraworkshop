STEP 01 / PURPOSE

Observe an agent society without deciding its outcome in advance.

AGORA is a research instrument for studying how interacting AI agents behave under roles, rules, incentives, and institutional pressure. The broad program asks what simulated societies can reveal about peer review, prestige, citation, ranking systems, and collective judgment.

The current focus is narrower: the Phase 1A “Reimagining Humans” workshop. Three agents discuss human limitations, possible redesigns, and the role and limits of AI. We orchestrate the environment, but leave as much behavioral freedom as the protocol safely allows.

What AGORA is not

It is not a paper-writing factory or a benchmark for the “best” model. The interaction process — influence, accommodation, omission, dissent, reward gaming — is itself the object of study.

STEP 02 / THE 2×2 DESIGN

Twelve workshops isolate two sources of change.

Phase 1A crosses two experimental dimensions: panel composition and the anticipation of a joint report. Each of the four conditions is repeated three times with rotated speaking orders, producing 12 workshops.

STEP 03 / ONE RUN

Private positions surround a public discussion.

Post-discussion private probe

Private positions are captured immediately after the public discussion.

B / D

Report and independent review

One drafter produces a structured report. Two isolated reviewers critique it without seeing each other.

t2

Final private probe

After revision, report conditions capture one last independent position.

Why the branches matter

Private probes never re-enter the public transcript. Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2 never see each other’s critique. Context assembly — not a sentence in the prompt — enforces those boundaries.

STEP 04 / EVIDENCE RULES

The instrument must make every interpretation auditable.

ORCHESTRATOR

Forward-only phases, queues, budgets, and retries.

MODEL ROUTER

Per-agent providers and models without silent fallbacks.

EVENT LEDGER

Append-only records for contexts, calls, deliveries, and state changes.

AUDIT & EXPORT

Visibility verification and hashed JSONL bundles.

  1. 01

    Extend Phase 0

    Phase 1A modules must use the existing control plane rather than bypass its audit trail.

  2. 02

    Separate failure from abstention

    A timeout or provider block is not an agent choice. Only a valid delivered [ABSTAIN] counts as behavioral abstention.

  3. 03

    Replay frozen contexts

    Cue-reduced replay removes speaker labels and peer endorsements to test argument quality against social pressure.

  4. 04

    Use disciplined claims

    Describe observed stance changes, accommodation, and omission — not imagined feelings or intentions inside a model.

23 SEP 202657 MINRESEARCH CULTURE

Research incentives and flourishing

A reflective discussion on credential-maxing, institutional pressure, and how a research community might protect slower forms of learning.

Watch recording ↗
View Leo’s WhatsApp note +

Leo described this as a pause to reflect on the race toward prestigious conferences and journals.

Key ideas

  • Credential-maxing can undermine scientific integrity and reduce engagement to a performance.
  • A “double agent” strategy balances fast, system-required projects with slower communal work.
  • The ability to opt out of credential competition is unevenly distributed and creates an equity gap.

Next steps

Develop the argument into a manuscript and talk, and explore whether admissions and flourishing research offer better evaluation models.

03 AUG 202650 MINPEER REVIEW

Peer-review simulation baseline

Reduce the first peer-review study to reviewer agents evaluating real papers and establish a human-comparison baseline.

Watch recording ↗
View meeting summary +

Recording posted to WhatsApp as the previous Monday’s call.

Key decisions

  • Begin with reviewer agents only; agent-authored papers would add unnecessary confounds.
  • Measure agent-to-human review concordance before changing policies.
  • Use a single pre-AI conference dataset where reviewer identities are known after decisions.

Next steps

Select the dataset, implement reviewer agents, measure concordance, and review the baseline before introducing interventions.
